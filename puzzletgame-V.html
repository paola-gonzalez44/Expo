Rompecabezas de Imagen

Arma la imagen tocando dos piezas para intercambiarlas de lugar.

Instrucciones

  1. Tap one piece.
  2. Tap another piece to swap them.
  3. Pieces in the correct place tunr green.
  4. Keep going until all the pieces are green.
  5. When all the pieces are green, the picture is complete!
