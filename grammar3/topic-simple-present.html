Simple Present Tense

Master the most essential verb tense in English. Learn to articulate daily routines, habits, facts, and general truths with absolute confidence.

Usage & Context

🔄 Habits & Routines

Actions that happen regularly or repeatedly in the present.

🌍 General Truths

Scientific facts, universal truths, and permanent states.

📍 Permanent Situations

Long-lasting states, professions, or places of residence.

📅 Scheduled Events

Timetables, programs, and fixed future arrangements.

✨

Sentence Structure

Positive Form
Subject + Verb + s / es (he, she, it)
Illustrative Examples
I work in a corporate office.
She works in a corporate office.
They play soccer on weekends.
Negative Form
Subject + do not / does not + Verb
Illustrative Examples
I don't like black coffee.
He doesn't work on Sundays.
We do not live in this city.
Interrogative Form
Do / Does + Subject + Verb + ?
Illustrative Examples
Do you speak English fluently?
Does she live near the office?
Do they meet every Monday?
⚠️

Spelling Rules & Exceptions

1

Sibilant Endings

For verbs ending in -s, -sh, -ch, -x, or -o, add -es.

go → goes · watch → watches · fix → fixes
2

Consonant + Y

Change the 'y' to 'i' and add -es.

study → studies · carry → carries · fly → flies
3

Vowel + Y

Simply add -s. The 'y' remains unchanged.

play → plays · buy → buys · enjoy → enjoys
4

The Irregular "Have"

The verb "have" changes to "has" for third-person singular.

I have a meeting · She has a meeting
