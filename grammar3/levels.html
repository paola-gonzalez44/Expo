Structured Learning Path

Progress through carefully designed levels aligned with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). Each level builds upon the previous one to ensure comprehensive skill development.

A1

Beginner Level

CEFR A1 - Foundation Building

Perfect for those starting their English journey. At this level, you'll develop essential communication skills for everyday situations, build a solid vocabulary foundation, and master basic grammar structures. You'll learn to introduce yourself, ask and answer simple questions, and interact in a straightforward manner.

Basic Communication

Introduce yourself and others, ask simple questions

📝

Essential Grammar

Present simple, basic verb tenses, articles

Listening Skills

Understand slow, clear speech on familiar topics

Reading Basics

Read short, simple texts and understand signs

Key Topics Covered:

Verb To Be Simple Present Basic Vocabulary Numbers & Time Daily Routines Family & People Places & Directions Food & Shopping

B1

Intermediate Level

CEFR B1 - Independent User

At the intermediate level, you'll gain confidence in handling most situations likely to arise in work, study, and social contexts. You'll develop the ability to express opinions, describe experiences, and understand the main points of complex texts. This level marks the transition from basic to independent language use.

Work & Business

Handle professional conversations and emails

✈️

Travel & Culture

Navigate travel situations and cultural exchanges

🎯

Complex Grammar

Perfect tenses, conditionals, modal verbs

🗣️

Fluent Speaking

Express ideas clearly on familiar topics

Key Topics Covered:

Present Perfect Past Continuous Future Forms First & Second Conditional Modal Verbs Reported Speech Business English Idioms & Phrases

B2

Upper Intermediate Level

CEFR B2 - Advanced Proficiency

Achieve fluency and spontaneity that makes regular interaction with native speakers effortless. At this level, you'll master complex language structures, understand nuanced meanings, and communicate effectively in professional and academic contexts. You'll be able to produce clear, detailed text on a wide range of subjects.

🎓

Academic English

Write essays, reports, and research papers

Professional Communication

Negotiate, present, and lead meetings

🎬

Native-Level Understanding

Understand films, literature, and media

✨

Nuanced Expression

Use idioms, phrasal verbs, and subtle meanings

Key Topics Covered:

Third Conditional Passive Voice (All Tenses) Advanced Phrasal Verbs Relative Clauses Subjunctive Mood Advanced Vocabulary Formal Writing Presentation Skills
